Vault #2

Nov 7th - Nov 13th, 2026

This week was packed, and most of it wasn't even code.

Saturday I had my Z Fellows interview with Cory Levy. It ran way over time, and honestly we barely talked about Centauri. It was mostly WALL-E, radio, space, and what I want to do long term. He asked me to send him these Vault entries, so hi Cory.

I've been signed up for NASA Space Apps for a while, but Monday night I finally registered my team. It's just me, as Centauri Lunar Atlas, and I'm doing the Lunar Landing Site Navigator challenge. My plan is a 3D Moon you can spin around the south pole, where every landing site gets scored on slope, how much sunlight it gets, and how close it is to the permanently shadowed craters where the ice might be. All of it comes from NASA's real LRO elevation and lighting maps, not made-up numbers. I start building from scratch tomorrow morning, fully solo, so we'll see how much one person can ship in a weekend.

On Centauri Academy, the big thing was GROL. Elements 1, 3, and 8 from the FCC pools are now in the app under a new Commercial Radio section, and the maritime card finally points to an actual course. I'm not shipping them until every question has an explanation, because that's the whole point of the site. Hopefully that's within a week or so. I also cleaned up the review flow: you can mark questions you guessed on, resume a half-finished session, and turn on auto-advance if you want it.

I also set up some outreach to ham radio clubs for the next couple weeks, so we'll see if anyone bites.

Off the computer, I got an A- on a math test covering stuff we'd literally just learned, wrote an English essay by hand, and did our school play tonight, which I think went pretty well. I missed the Flight 15 launch because of school, but it's in orbit right now, which is still insane.

Next week is mostly Space Apps and the GROL explanations.
