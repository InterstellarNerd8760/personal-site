Vault entry #1: Starting the log

Oct 31st - Nov 6th, 2026

I build a lot of stuff, and I hardly write any of it down. So this is me trying to fix that. The idea is that each week, I'll post what I did that week.

How I got here. I got my ham license (KO6OGZ) and built Centauri Academy, a free study app for the FCC ham exams. It has hundreds of questions from the official pools, explanations, and drills for your weak areas. It became an actual product and a growing habit, and now I'm trying to document the work instead of just doing it. Right now it's the main thing, but I don't want it to be the main thing forever. I'm also working on WALL-E, a high-altitude balloon with its own flight computer and LoRa radio. NASA Space Apps is coming up, and I run cross country.

This week.

  • The Ham Question of the Day Chrome extension is live (Chrome Web Store), with 95 installs in its first few days, so that's pretty freaking cool. It gives you one exam question a day in a popup and on your new tab page.
  • There's now an "Add to Chrome" card on the Centauri homepage.
  • If you study while signed out and then make an account, your progress comes with you.
  • I added privacy-friendly analytics, rate limiting on the server, SEO and mobile speed work, and a fix for a cookie banner that wouldn't respond to clicks.
  • Searching "Centauri Academy" on Google brings up my site first now, above Centauri Arts Academy.
  • 50 extra questions got answered on Centauri today.
  • The Centauri+ waitlist got its first signup that wasn't my own school email.
  • I pulled out the WALL-E flight computer and started on the wiring. It's not working yet, which is unfortunate, but I hope to get it working by the end of the year, ahead of launch in spring.
  • I did more planning for Space Apps.

Off the keyboard. I ran my last cross-country race of the season and ran a PR. We were trying to get under 18 and didn't, but it was still a fast race (I believe it was around 19:11-19:13). It was a good way to close out the season. A friend and I split a Peach-flavored Red Bull on the way there, and I crushed the can after. It's getting 3D scanned, so it'll have its own page soon at /can.

Next week: NASA Space Apps prep, getting WALL-E1's flight computer talking, and keeping on getting more people to try Centauri. The hackathon itself is the weekend after (Nov 14–15).
