Vault entry #1: Starting the log

Nov 6, 2026

I build a lot of stuff, and I hardly write any of it down. So this is me trying to fix that. The idea is that each week, I’ll post what I did.

How I got here: I got my ham license (KO6OGZ) and built Centauri Academy, a free study app for the FCC ham exams. It became an actual product and a growing habit, and now I’m trying to document the work instead of just doing it.

This week.

  • The Ham Question of the Day Chrome extension is live (install it here).
  • There’s now an “Add to Chrome” card on the Centauri homepage.
  • If you study while signed out and then create an account, your progress comes with you.
  • I added privacy-friendly analytics, rate limiting on the server, SEO and mobile speed work, and a fix for a cookie banner that wouldn’t respond to taps.
  • Searching for “Centauri Academy” on Google now brings up my site first, ahead of a different Centauri Arts Academy.
  • 50 extra questions were answered on Centauri today.
  • The Centauri+ waitlist got its first signup that wasn’t from my own school email.
  • I pulled the WALL-E flight computer out and started wiring it up. It still isn’t working yet, and I’m fighting it again this weekend.
  • I did more planning for Space Apps.

Off the keyboard. I ran my last cross-country race of the season and set a PR. We were trying to get under 18 minutes and didn’t, but it was still a fast race; I believe it was around 19:11–19:12. It was a good way to close out the season.

Next week: NASA Space Apps prep, getting WALL-E’s flight computer talking, and continuing to get more people to try Centauri. The hackathon itself is the weekend after (Nov 14–15).
