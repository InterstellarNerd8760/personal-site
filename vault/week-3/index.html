A Weekend on the Moon

Nov 14th - Nov 20th, 2026 · Entry #3

This week started with my first hackathon.

Saturday morning I started building Centauri Lunar Atlas from scratch, solo, for the Lunar Landing Site Navigator challenge at NASA Space Apps. It's a 3D Moon in the browser, using NASA's real LOLA elevation and LROC imagery. You can pick a landing site and see how the actual terrain horizon affects sunlight and whether Earth is in view, compare up to three sites, or fly around and walk on the surface. I submitted it at 11:48 PM Pacific on Sunday. Judging runs through the 22nd, and global nominees come out December 3rd. It's at moon.westonrosch.com if you want to look around. This was extremely fun, and I definitely want to do more hackathons, so hit me up if you know of any.

Also Saturday, Flight 15 came back, and I took a little break from the hackathon to watch it. The ship got caught at the tower, the first time they've caught the upper stage. The booster didn't make it back, but holy crap, the ship catch was amazing. Really looking forward to the next flight. I want to see both stages get caught, hopefully, and more operational flights. Go Starship.

On Centauri Academy, I finished explanations for every GROL question (Elements 1, 3, and 8: 144, 600, and 300). That track goes live tomorrow at noon Pacific, and I spent $5 on an Instagram ad for it, so we'll see how that goes. I also emailed some local ham clubs, and I actually got feedback from a blind ham who uses a screen reader. I started fixing the practice exams almost immediately. That work is still in progress.

I also put a Lunar Atlas project page and a seven-slide demo on my site.

Off the computer, Thanksgiving break started, and I kind of stumbled into basketball. I sat in on one practice, they said they needed a manager, and I said maybe I could do that. Friday I helped at the varsity boys' game, mostly passing water and cheering. It's unpaid. I'm thinking about joining JV and maybe playing in Tuesday's first game, but I'm worried I'm bad enough to drag the team down. I've been out shooting hoops, extremely badly.

Next week is the GROL launch, and waiting on Space Apps judging.
