05 / Method & scope

Built from what's known.

This observatory covers my account only. The reported analytics and activity were supplied by me in November 2026; they are not retrieved live from X. Public display does not imply any official X integration. There are no visitor uploads, other people's accounts, or speculative network clusters.

For maintainability, the public numbers live in one small source snapshot. I can add future observations without replacing the entire design. Individual account relationships are omitted until a reliable dataset exists.
