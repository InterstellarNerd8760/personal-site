    05 / Method & scope

    Built from public posts.

    I looked up the public profile for @rosch_weston, then paged through every post of mine and every public @mention of me between 12:00 AM PT on Nov 5 and 10:26 AM PT on Nov 19, 2026. The API returns at most 25 posts per request, so the window was collected page by page. Account metrics on the graph come from the user objects returned with those posts.

    A counted interaction is one public post in that window that ties my account to another: I replied to, mentioned, quoted, or reposted them, or they replied to, mentioned, or quoted me. Each such post adds 1 to that account's weight. Other people mentioned in the same post are also counted once. Self-replies are omitted. Likes, follows, and DMs are not counted.

    Clusters are about direction, not invented friend groups. Mutual means replies, mentions, or quotes went both ways. Outbound means I reached out and they did not reply in this window. Inbound is the reverse. Amplify means I only reposted them.

    I also checked an earlier Sorsa follower snapshot that had listed 10 accounts. That file was not in the repo, but the handles from the previous page were. Seven of them show up in this window: @space_jm_, @astro_kep, @mathsboi42, @Miss1onControl, @Hugh__Mann, @ApoStructura, and @Echo5550. @GoodAaron, @RickAddante, and @farzyness had no counted public interactions here, so they are not drawn as live nodes.

    What I could not get: a verified-follower count (that field is not authorized on this lookup), follower or following lists, and X Analytics private totals such as profile visits. The snapshot can be rebuilt with build-atlas.py from a folder of public lookup dumps; the page itself only reads x-atlas.json.
