05 / Interpretation

What the numbers actually say.

Two useful ratios from the approximate one-year totals. Neither describes unique people, follower conversion, or the performance of a specific post.

Engagements / impressions

~1.8%

Approximately 8.6K engagements divided by 471.4K impressions in the reported period. An interaction-to-impression ratio, not a measure of distinct engaged people.

Same approximate annual window

Profile visits / impressions

~0.25%

Approximately 1.2K profile visits divided by 471.4K impressions. This is a descriptive ratio, not a verified click-through or visitor conversion rate.

Same approximate annual window

Observed signal

The reported daily reach rose from roughly 1K impressions to several days around 4–5K, with one day near 6K. Replies reportedly account for much of that reach. These are notable observations, but not a complete daily dataset.

Unanswered questions

Which original posts bring people back? How many profile visits lead to a meaningful conversation or a visit to my projects? Which connections persist? The present snapshot cannot answer these questions.

Next measurements

From impressions to impact.

  • Record weekly dated follower and engagement snapshots, with their sources.
  • Compare original posts and replies instead of treating all impressions alike.
  • Track visits to my projects separately from X analytics where measurement is available.
  • Expand the account map only with verifiable links and observations.

06 / Method & scope

Built from what's known.

This observatory covers my account only. The reported analytics and activity were supplied by me in November 2026; they are not retrieved live from X. Public display does not imply any official X integration. The account nodes are selected historical observations, not a complete or live network.

For maintainability, the public numbers live in one small source snapshot. I can add future observations without replacing the entire design. A small set of documented account relationships is included; all other relationships remain unknown.
