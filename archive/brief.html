CENTAURI ARCHIVE / PROJECT BRIEF / PROTOTYPE 0.6.1

A record of a living world.

An independent, open-source effort to preserve foundational knowledge of Earth, its biosphere and human civilization in a compact record for interstellar travel.

Research prototype. No mission affiliation, approved payload, flight-qualified carrier or independently validated decoding method.

Read the prototype collection ↗View the source ↗

01 / THE PURPOSE

Preserve what matters.

A compact archive must make hard choices: what to explain, what to show, whose perspectives to include, and what survives if its original creators cannot help interpret it.

The initial editorial plan begins with location, matter, life, environment, humanity and attributed personal messages. The collection is incomplete and not yet scientifically reviewed.

02 / BUILT TODAY

A verifiable prototype.

  • Original introductory records across science, Earth, life and humanity; three original scientific diagrams.
  • Deterministic digital packaging and versioned metadata.
  • SHA-256 corruption detection and automated verification tests.
  • A small interactive image-decoding experiment and a multimedia-capable human reading interface.

03 / THE PROPOSED ARTIFACT

From bits to matter.

A passive etched carrier is one design candidate. It would pair inspectable decoding examples with microscopic information that does not require the spacecraft's electronics to remain operational.

Electronic storage may supplement it if a durable readout method and meaningful redundancy can be demonstrated. No physical medium has been selected or fabricated.

04 / THE MISSION CONTEXT

A potential contribution.

Fermi Explorer is one potential mission context, not a partner or an approved host. The project's intent is to investigate a modern scientific companion to historic cultural archives such as Voyager's Golden Record.

Any flight proposal would depend on verified mission constraints, appropriate contributors and evidence from physical experiments.

NEXT DEVELOPMENT GATE

Prove the reading method.

Test an independently recoverable, self-contained physical primer example, have independent readers attempt reconstruction without instructions, document failures, and revise. In parallel, build a small linked scientific collection with sources, diagrams and editorial review. Only then evaluate carrier dimensions and environmental tests against actual mission requirements.
