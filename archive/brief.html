Project brief

Centauri Archive is an independent, open-source effort to preserve a compact record of Earth, life and human civilization. A self-describing, passive archive could give an interstellar spacecraft a record that can be read after onboard power is lost.

Development status. Software prototype. The Fermi Explorer concept is an independent proposal for feasibility review. Centauri Archive has no flight approval or mission allocation.

What exists today

A reproducible development package contains credited photographs, encyclopedia drafts, diagrams and preliminary primer material. Independent software decoders reconstruct sample photographs. Controlled tests recover records from damaged digital samples within documented limits.

How it could fly

An engraved exterior primer could point to finer markings and a protected passive carrier, potentially with independent copies. Readout would begin with orientation, counting and reference images, then progress to protected digital records. The carrier has yet to be fabricated or tested.

What technical review could resolve

Could the mission accommodate a passive demonstrator? A technical point of contact and guidance on placement, mass, volume, loads, temperature, radiation and impact exposure would help define a testable design. The next step would be to fabricate marked samples and measure readout, damage recovery and first-read interpretation.
