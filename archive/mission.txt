# Mission fit — open questions

**Status:** This is an independent proposal. See [the dated PSI and public-source brief](fermi-mission-sources-2026.md) for the conflicting July study, current official FAQ, and calculator assumptions.

 Fermi Explorer has not approved,
requested or endorsed Centauri Archive. Launch year, trajectory, spacecraft
dimensions, payload mass and any ability to host an archive must be confirmed
directly with the mission team. A previously discussed 2029 target is an
aspiration, not a verified engineering specification for this project.

To avoid accidentally designing the wrong hardware, request:

1. Allowed payload mass, external dimensions, center-of-mass and mounting.
2. Whether there is an allocation for passive objects, and whether another
   archive or cultural payload is already planned.
3. Vehicle configuration, trajectory, speed, and expected mission phases.
4. Peak launch acceleration, vibration and shock, and expected thermal cycles.
5. Radiation and micrometeoroid environment assumptions and shielding.
6. Electrical power, data interface, onboard storage and telemetry allocation,
   if any — none should be assumed.
7. Time for integration, environmental testing, documentation and approval.
8. Handling cleanliness, outgassing, licensing and safety requirements.
9. Whether contribution requires a formal team, university or sponsor.

Archive's **Fermi Edition** should only be sized and built against approved
values. Its **Ark Edition** is a separate future concept and is not a claim
about Fermi Explorer capabilities.
