A MESSAGE FROM EARTH / UNAPPROVED EDITORIAL DRAFT

This is placeholder editorial text for discussion, not an approved message
from Weston Rosch, Fermi Explorer or humanity as a whole.

We are one species among many living forms on a small planet orbiting a star.
Across generations we have observed our surroundings, learned to describe
parts of the universe, and built tools to carry our questions beyond Earth.

This record is incomplete. It reflects some knowledge and choices available
to its makers, not the full diversity of Earth's lives and cultures.

We hope a future reader can learn something of our planet and the life within
it. The record's existence should not be mistaken for a claim to ownership
of the places beyond our world.

Future editions should include contributions from many people and a carefully
reviewed personal message from the project's founder.
