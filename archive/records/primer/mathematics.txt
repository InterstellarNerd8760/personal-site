PRIMER 01 / NUMBERS AND REPEATED STRUCTURE
Human-readable explanation of ideas planned for a physical, language-independent primer.

COUNTING: zero, one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight.
DECIMAL: 0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8.
BINARY:  0 1 10 11 100 101 110 111 1000.
TWO IS REPRESENTED BY ONE GROUP OF TWO OBJECTS.
THREE IS REPRESENTED BY ONE GROUP OF THREE OBJECTS.

In the future physical primer, these relationships must be demonstrated through
repeated geometric marks and correspondences, not English or our digit symbols.

Some properties are independent of the arbitrary names we use:
2 + 3 = 5; 2 x 3 = 6; 2 x 2 = 4.
2, 3, 5, 7, 11 and 13 are the first six prime numbers.
An asymmetric marker can distinguish the beginning and orientation of a grid.

This human-readable file documents the mathematical concepts; it does not
establish that a future observer will interpret the marks as intended.
