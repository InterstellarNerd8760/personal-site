HUMANITY / A VERY SHORT CHRONOLOGY

Time labels in this list use the human Gregorian calendar and are not
universal physical references. Years are approximate unless noted.

- Anatomically modern Homo sapiens arose in Africa hundreds of thousands
  of years ago.
- Independent forms of agriculture developed in multiple regions beginning
  roughly twelve thousand years ago, with substantial regional variation.
- Writing systems emerged in several ancient societies, including in
  Mesopotamia and Egypt more than five thousand years ago.
- Printing, mathematics, medicine, astronomy and navigation developed through
  contributions across many societies and centuries.
- Industrialization transformed energy use, production and transport in parts
  of the world beginning in the eighteenth century, with global consequences.
- Radio and electronic computing expanded communication and calculation in
  the nineteenth and twentieth centuries.
- Sputnik 1 became the first artificial satellite to orbit Earth in 1957.
- Humans first landed on Earth's Moon in 1969.

This record omits far more than it includes. It is a table of contents for
future research and review, not an authoritative account of all societies.
