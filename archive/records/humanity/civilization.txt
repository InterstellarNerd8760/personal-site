HUMANITY / WHO WE ARE

Humans (Homo sapiens) are a species of social, tool-making primates. Human
individuals are born, learn, form relationships, cooperate and disagree.
We communicate through speech, signed languages, writing, images, music,
mathematics and technologies. Thousands of spoken and signed languages exist.

Human cultures vary across time and place. There is no single way of living
that represents every person. Our communities have created families,
agriculture, cities, trade networks, institutions, arts, religious and
philosophical traditions, sciences, and engineering. They have also known
inequality, conflict, environmental damage and loss.

Our shared biology does not imply a single shared political system or set
of beliefs. A record of humanity should include everyday life and diverse
contributions as well as exceptional technologies or achievements.

This small text is an orientation, not a comprehensive account. Future curated
records need global participation, provenance, context, translations,
licenses and consent where applicable.
