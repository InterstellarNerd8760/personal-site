Water is a chemical compound. Each water molecule ordinarily consists of
two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom: H2O.

Water exists naturally on Earth as liquid, solid ice and vapor. At a pressure
of about 101325 pascals, pure water freezes near 273.15 kelvin and boils
near 373.15 kelvin. Phase changes depend on pressure and dissolved material.
The physical primer must independently define these human measurement units.

Liquid water covers most of Earth's surface, especially in the oceans.
Water also occurs in clouds, groundwater, rivers, lakes, ice and organisms.
Evaporation, condensation, precipitation and runoff transport water and
energy through Earth's environment.

Water is a solvent for many substances and participates in essential
chemical reactions in known terrestrial life. Its importance here does not
prove that all possible extraterrestrial life requires water.
