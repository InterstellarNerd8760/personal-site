About the imagery

Not every image represents a completed landing. Orbital images show spacecraft locations and candidate terrain; mission photographs show hardware or descent perspectives. Artist concepts of future lunar landers, including the Blue Moon HLS, are illustrative only and do not identify the actual spacecraft, final landing site, or existence of a base at any candidate region.

Commercial mission photographs and concept art remain credited to their creators. Some images are externally hosted and may not support embedding; the source link is provided for each. The existing 37 lunar-site photographs are stored with the open-source project.
